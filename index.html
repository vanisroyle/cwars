How to Play

Goal: capture every building on the map before your enemies do.

Send troops: press the mouse on one of your buildings, drag over more of your buildings to add them to the order, then release on the target. Each building sends half of its garrison.

Houses grow population over time (up to their soft cap). Shrines generate crystal energy for spells and send fast cavalry. Towers shoot arrows at passing enemies and send heavy infantry worth two defenders each.

Combat: when troops reach an enemy building they fight until one side is gone. Bigger armies and better Attack/Defense stats win.

Spells: click a spell icon, then click the map. Spells cost crystal energy — capture shrines to earn it faster.

Upgrades: victories earn XP. Spend it on stats and stronger spells between battles.
